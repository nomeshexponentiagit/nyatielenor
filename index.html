Welcome to

The Handcrafted Life

Nestled in the serene locale of Lulla Nagar, South Pune, Nyati Elenor stands as a testament to architectural brilliance. Spread across 2.5 acres, these 32-storey twin towers house meticulously designed 3.5, 4.5 & 5.5 BHK residences, each a masterpiece of space and luxury.

With sizes ranging from 1,798 to 2,757 sq.ft, every home at Nyati Elenor is crafted to offer an unparalleled living experience, blending contemporary design with timeless elegance.
